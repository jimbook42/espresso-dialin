# Espresso Dial-In Android privacy policy

Espresso Dial-In for Android is published by Tull Labs. This policy takes effect when it is published for the Android release. For privacy questions, contact TullLabs@outlook.com.

## Information kept on your device

Espresso Dial-In works without an app account. It stores your shots, notes, beans, recipes, settings, and saved sensor readings on your device. The camera reader processes video on the device to read the pressure gauge. Saved shots can include a gauge image. A backup can also contain the latest replay if one was previously saved or restored. Android stores permission choices separately. The app remembers the name and Bluetooth address of your last connected scale for that scale profile so it can reconnect.

Camera and Nearby Devices are optional. The camera permission lets you use pressure tracking. Nearby Devices lets the app discover and communicate with a Bluetooth scale. Older Android versions may ask for Location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices. Espresso Dial-In does not use it to determine where you are. You can change permissions in Android Settings and log shots manually without them.

## Backups, diagnostics and contributions

A backup is saved to the location you choose. It may contain personal notes, recordings and settings. The backup file is unencrypted. Keep it somewhere private and share it only with someone you choose. If you choose a cloud folder or another app, that service handles the file under its own terms.

The app keeps a limited local diagnostic history to help you report problems. It keeps up to 120 entries for up to seven days and removes older entries when the log is used. Reports can include the app version and build, Android version, recent app, camera and Bluetooth events, JavaScript error categories and summary counts. By default, reports exclude private shot notes, device addresses, recorded samples and media. You can add a description and separately choose to include the device model or detailed shot information where available. The app shows what will go into the file. The report is sent only if you choose to share it and complete delivery using the recipient app.

Contributing shots is optional. The app lets you preview selected brew measurements, bean and grinder settings, taste ratings, sensor samples, and information about the calculation, source and quality of readings. The file omits private notes, saved shot IDs, device addresses, photos and video. It includes a random export ID and can include a tester alias you supply. If you send it to us, we use it to assess and improve Espresso Dial-In's shot analysis and recommendations. An email you send may identify you even if the file does not include your name.

The Android app does not automatically send your shots, camera images, diagnostic reports or contributions to us. Vercel Web Analytics is disabled in the Android version covered by this policy. The hosted web app has a separate analytics preference.

## Support, retention and deletion

If you email support or submit a report or contribution, we receive the email address, message and files you send. We use support submissions to reply and investigate issues. We use contributions to evaluate and improve the app. Our support mailbox provider processes messages on our behalf. The email, storage or sharing app you choose can also handle your attachments.

Tull Labs keeps diagnostic reports and their related support correspondence for up to 90 days from receipt. Contributed shot data and the submission records needed to manage it are kept for up to 12 months from receipt. We review and remove expired material manually. Access to submissions is limited to people handling support or contribution review for Tull Labs.

To ask about, access, correct or request deletion of material you sent to Tull Labs, email TullLabs@outlook.com. Include the approximate submission date or contribution export ID if you have it. We may ask for enough information to identify the submission. We can act on the copies we control. Your email or sharing provider and other recipients manage their own copies.

You can delete shots and beans in the app and clear recent diagnostic events from Settings → Report a problem. Clearing Android app storage erases local app records. Keep a verified backup before you remove anything you want to retain. Files exported outside the app are managed separately.
